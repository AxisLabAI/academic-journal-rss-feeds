## Read the evidence correctly / 怎样理解证据

- Platform policy: the publisher documents a feature, not a measured count of healthy feeds.
- Official list: a count of named titles or explicitly linked paths, with units preserved.
- Journal example: evidence for one title, not every title of that publisher.
- Historical policy: evidence may no longer describe the current platform.
- Not established: further investigation needed; not proof that RSS is absent.

## Source notes / 来源与口径

### Elsevier

综合学科；含 Cell Press、The Lancet 等品牌。

Scale: 3,000+. Publisher portfolio; not a title-by-title active-journal census. [Official source](https://www.elsevier.com/en-au/open-access).

RSS: ScienceDirect documents RSS for all journal issue and book series alerts, not search RSS. Policy scope is the platform; no complete title census. [Official source](https://www.elsevier.support/sciencedirect/answer/whats-the-difference-between-a-sciencedirect-alert-and-rss-feed).

All-portfolio official RSS total: unknown. Self-build-required total: unknown.

### Springer Nature

综合学科；Nature、Springer、BMC 不重复统计。

Scale: 3,000+. Group portfolio, 2025 OA report. Nature and BMC are not added again. [Official source](https://stories.springernature.com/oa-report-2025/delivering-value/).

RSS: Nature journal homepage explicitly links an RSS feed. This is journal-level evidence, not a group-wide policy. [Official source](https://www.nature.com/).

All-portfolio official RSS total: unknown. Self-build-required total: unknown.

### Taylor & Francis

综合学科；Routledge、Dovepress、PeerJ 属集团品牌。

Scale: 2,500+. Company-published portfolio. The separate platform claim of 2,700+ is not interchangeable. [Official source](https://taylorandfrancis.com/about/).

RSS: Official 2019 user guide describes RSS alerts. Historical support evidence, not proof of present coverage. [Official source](https://librarianresources.taylorandfrancis.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/USER-GUIDE-tandfonline.pdf).

All-portfolio official RSS total: unknown. Self-build-required total: unknown.

### Wiley Online Library

综合学科；本次数字为平台馆藏口径。

Scale: 2,000+. Platform collection count; not deduplicated current publisher-owned active journals. [Official source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/).

RSS: Official researcher guide describes TOC and Early View RSS via Journal Tools. Complete current coverage not enumerated. [Official source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/researchers/read/find-research).

All-portfolio official RSS total: unknown. Self-build-required total: unknown.

### Sage

人文社科及理工医；官网数字存在口径差异。

Scale: 1,100+. Press office states 1,100 journals; older journal landing text also says 1,440 portfolio / 1,100+ browse. Conflict retained; no reconciled precise count. [Official source](https://www.sagepub.com/explore-our-content/press-office).

RSS: Official Sage RSS help states Recent Articles and OnlineFirst feeds are available for all hosted titles. Platform policy, not a live title census. [Official source](https://journals.sagepub.com/connected/jou).

All-portfolio official RSS total: unknown. Self-build-required total: unknown.

## Global benchmark / 全球总量

The [STM Global Brief 2021](https://stm-assoc.org/document/stm-global-brief-2021-economics-and-market-size-2/), page 15, cites more than 48,000 active peer-reviewed scholarly journals in all languages from Ulrichsweb, accessed 19 September 2021. This is a historical benchmark, not a 2026 census and not an RSS coverage denominator. 本次没有得到可核实的 2026 年全球活跃期刊精确总数、官方 RSS 总数或需自建总数。

A future census must include smaller publishers, scholarly societies, university presses and regional/language-specific journals. This review does not yet cover Wolters Kluwer, De Gruyter Brill, Emerald, RSC, IOP, APS and many independent journals in the same depth.
